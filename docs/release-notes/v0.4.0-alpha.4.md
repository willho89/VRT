Fourth alpha of VRT for Meta Quest 2 and Quest 3. **Download `VRT-0.4.0-alpha.4.apk` below**; one APK for both headsets. It installs over earlier alphas and keeps your settings and saves.

### New
- **Live TV** on the new **TV** channel, through [X-Streemly](https://github.com/willho89/X-Streemly) (bundled). Pick **LIVE TV** and it finds an **HDHomeRun** tuner on your network by itself (ATSC channels as 3.1, 3.2 …), or else plays [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv)'s free public channels for the country in its options (default UK). Your own channel lists (`.m3u`) or single stream URLs (`.strm` files holding one URL) go in `/sdcard/VRT/tv/`. VRT and X-Streemly host no streams.
- On the TV channel: **Up / Down** changes channel (the tune waits until you stop flicking), **B** goes back to the last channel, **A** shows the channel info, **Select** opens the channel list, **X / Y** mark and jump to favourites, **L / R** go to the previous / next group. The channel number and name appear on the picture, like a real TV's.
- The TV picture comes in at standard definition (480 lines), the way a CRT TV would show a broadcast, whatever the stream's own resolution.

### Known issues
- **Live TV is new and so far only tested on the desktop build**, not yet in the headset. If it stutters, goes silent or won't find your tuner, please report it.
- Interlaced broadcasts are deinterlaced in the core for now, not yet drawn field by field like DVDs.
- No keyboard on the headset, so channels are picked with Up/Down or the channel list, not by number.
- Many iptv-org streams are geo-blocked or offline at any moment; channels that don't answer are skipped for a day.
- PAL (50 Hz) video can't pace perfectly evenly: Quest has no 100 Hz mode.
- Alpha: settings and formats may still change.

### Install (new users)
Developer mode, then sideload with [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/) or `adb install`. Open VRT from **Unknown sources**, allow **All files access**, and stay online the first time you use each system (emulator cores download from the libretro buildbot). VRT ships no games or videos; see the [README](https://github.com/willho89/VRT#readme).
