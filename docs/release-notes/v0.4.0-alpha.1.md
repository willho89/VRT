First public alpha of VRT, a faithfully emulated CRT TV in VR and AR, for Meta Quest 2 and Quest 3.

**Download `VRT-0.4.0-alpha.1.apk` below.** The same APK works on Quest 2 and Quest 3; VRT detects a Quest 2 and uses lighter defaults (panel-native resolution, up to 90 Hz).

### Install
1. Put the headset in developer mode.
2. Sideload the APK with [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/) (drag it in) or `adb install VRT-0.4.0-alpha.1.apk`.
3. Open VRT from **Unknown sources** in the app library and allow **All files access** when asked.
4. Keep the headset online the first time you use each system. VRT downloads the emulator cores from the official libretro buildbot (a few MB each, once).

### Bring your own content
VRT ships with no games or videos.
- ROMs: `/sdcard/VRT/roms/<System>/` (NES, SNES, Genesis / Mega Drive, Master System, Game Gear, PC Engine; zips are fine)
- DVDs: `/sdcard/VRT/video/` (`.iso`, `.chd`, or `VIDEO_TS` folders). Decrypted rips play as-is. For CSS-encrypted images, supply `libdvdcss.so.2` yourself in `/sdcard/VRT/system/deevee/`.

### Controls
- **Menu button**, Select+Start on a gamepad: the on-TV guide. Point at the TV and pull the trigger, or use the d-pad/stick.
- Guide → **VRT** channel → **TV menu**: volume, picture, TV type, input (RGB / S-Video / composite / RF), signal strength, overscan, detail, resolution, refresh rate.
- **Right grip** on the TV: move it (the sticks push/pull, turn and tilt; add the left grip to scale).
- **Left grip + sticks**: walk around the VR room.

### What's in this alpha
- The CRT: phosphor masks you can put your face against, scanlines, true interlace for DVDs, halation, curved glass.
- NTSC composite / S-Video / RF signal emulation with per-console timing; TV picture controls.
- Four TV types (25" TV/VCR combo, 27" aperture grille, 14" broadcast monitor, 13" portable). Add your own in `/sdcard/VRT/tvs/` ([format](https://github.com/willho89/VRT/blob/main/docs/tv-types.md)).
- VR room, or passthrough AR on Quest 3 with the TV pinned to your real room, occlusion and light spill.
- Sound from the TV's speakers in 3D, save states, recents and favourites.

### Known issues
- Alpha: settings and save formats may change between versions.
- The APK is signed with a development key. If a later release ever changes the key, you'll need to uninstall first (your settings and saves would be lost).
- Quest 2 performance is being tuned. If it stutters, lower **RES** or **REFRESH** in the TV menu, and please report the fps shown on the floating panel (guide → VRT → Floating panel).
