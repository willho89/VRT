Third alpha of VRT for Meta Quest 2 and Quest 3. **Download `VRT-0.4.0-alpha.3.apk` below**; one APK for both headsets. It installs over earlier alphas and keeps your settings and saves.

### New
- **PlayStation** support (PCSX ReARMed; runs on Quest 2 too). Put games in `/sdcard/VRT/roms/PlayStation/` (or `PS/`). A BIOS is optional: `/sdcard/VRT/system/pcsx_rearmed/scph5501.bin`.
- **VHS channel** for ordinary video files (mp4, mkv, avi, mov, webm…) in `/sdcard/VRT/vhs/`, through RetroArch's FFmpeg core. When a video ends, the guide comes back.
- **Tape** (TV menu → TAPE: AUTO / OFF / GOOD): a VCR in the signal chain. Soft tape detail, edge halos, smeared colour, grain, slight wobble, the torn head-switching band at the bottom, plus tape hiss, duller sound and wow & flutter. AUTO plays the VHS channel from tape and everything else direct.
- **Glass on the tube** (TV menu → GLASS): a highlight that slides across the curved glass as you move, an edge sheen, subtle scratches and wipe marks (each TV type has its own amount), and real reflections of the VR room.
- **AR LIGHT** (TV menu): turn the CRT's glow on your real room on or off.

### Improved
- **CRT detail you can see from the couch**: the new default **DISTANCE** level keeps scanlines visible from about 3 ft without the mask turning into rainbow moiré. Levels: CLEAN, STRONG, DISTANCE, EXAGGERATED, RAW.
- **Sharper RGB**: the RGB input gives crisp pixels with scanlines; S-Video sits in between; composite and RF stay soft.
- Switching TV TYPE no longer resets your input.

### Known issues
- N64 isn't supported: it was too slow in the headset.
- PAL (50 Hz) video can't pace perfectly evenly: Quest has no 100 Hz mode.
- Alpha: settings and formats may still change.

### Install (new users)
Developer mode, then sideload with [SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/) or `adb install`. Open VRT from **Unknown sources**, allow **All files access**, and stay online the first time you use each system (emulator cores download from the libretro buildbot). VRT ships no games or videos; see the [README](https://github.com/willho89/VRT#readme).
